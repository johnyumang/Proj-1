# Prompt 2 — SIIL's own models: sheets (Filipino, dark and kayumanggi)

**How to use:** these prompts go into your image generator, one at a time. For every prompt, paste the **style block** (section 3), then that model's **identity block** (section 2), then the **sheet prompt** (section 4). Where a prompt says "attach", upload the listed real SIIL photos as reference images.
Do the sheets in order. Sheet 1 creates the person; every later sheet uses Sheet 1 as its reference image so the face and body stay the same.

The same shot list works as a brief for a real photo shoot with real Filipino models, if you decide to shoot instead of generate.

## 1. Rules

1. **Adults only, invented people.** No real person's name or likeness, no celebrity look-alikes.
2. **Models are illustrations.** Never label a generated model as a customer, a "real ostomate", a patient or a reviewer, on the site or in ads.
3. **The belt is never invented.** Any image that shows the belt is generated with the real product photos attached, and is checked against them (section 6) before use.
4. **Close-ups of the product are real photographs.** The access opening, the zip, the label and the fabric are photographed, not generated.
5. **No pouch, stoma, scar or medical setting** in generated images. The belt lies flat with a slight soft fullness on one side of the front panel.
6. **Skin stays as described.** No lightening, no smoothing to plastic, no narrowing of features. Reject any result that drifts paler than the target.

## 3. Style block (start every prompt with this)

```
Photorealistic studio photograph, full-frame camera, 85 mm lens, f/5.6, eye-level.
Seamless neutral mid-grey backdrop, evenly lit, with a soft shadow at the feet.
Large soft key light from the front left, gentle fill from the right, a narrow warm rim light from behind so the hair, shoulders and skin edge separate clearly from the backdrop.
Skin is rendered rich and true to tone with natural texture: visible pores, no beauty retouching, no glow filter.
Natural, realistic adult body proportions. Hands relaxed with five fingers each.
No jewellery, no tattoos, no logos, no text, no props, no other people.
```

### Motion clips for the scrubbed sequences (image-to-video; start frame is the matching stage plate)

Each clip: 4 seconds, locked-off camera, no zoom, no cuts, constant lighting, plain backdrop, the person stays centred and fully in frame.

```
fit:     The person turns slowly and smoothly in place from facing the camera to a full side profile, feet almost planted, arms relaxed. Nothing else moves.
clothes: The person lowers the hem of the T-shirt over the belt with both hands and smooths it flat, then lets the arms drop. Slow, natural.
pool:    The person takes two slow steps toward the camera and stops, water drops running off the shoulders. The belt does not shift.
hero:    Almost still: one slow breath, a slight shift of weight, the shirt edge moves a little.
```

Export every frame, remove the background so each frame has transparency, save as WebP at 1080 px tall (and a 720 px set), and number them `0001.webp` upward in `public/media/belt/seq/<chapter>/<color>/`.

## 6. Check every image before it is used

**Person**
- Same face, hairline, identifying mark and body as Sheet 1.
- Skin matches the target tone under this lighting, with texture. Not paler, not grey, not orange.
- On a dark page the figure still reads clearly: skin, hair and a black belt do not merge into the background.
- Hands and feet are correct. Nothing extra in frame.

**Belt** (compare side by side with the real photo)
- Wide band, top edge at the navel, rounded front panel curving down at the lower edge.
- Top binding present; one small label on the upper front; no buckles, straps, lacing or printed logos.
- Colour matches the real beige, navy or black.
- Lies flat. No visible pouch, tube or outline of a device.
- If any point fails, regenerate. Do not fix a wrong belt with words on the page.
